# The audit of 22.11.2026: bugs, duplicated code, fixed values to give to users

Ten checks in parallel, read-only (7 on the engine, 3 on the interface), each told not to repeat [the audit of 15.11](audit-2026-11-15.md).

A note on the references:
- Nine of the checks read the branch `feat/terrain-82-3d-subregions` (4f8250573), which is 254 commits behind `main`.
- Structure (Lapin.Analysis, Rebar, Steel, Wood) was read on `main` (789c065b8).
- The serious findings were checked again on `main` and are still there. Line numbers may be off by a few dozen lines on `main`; the function names are right.
- Those marked "unconfirmed" at the end were not checked to the end.

## 1. In short

- **Bugs:** about 110 confirmed by reading. One is serious, about 35 are of medium severity, the rest are small.
- **Three causes recur** and explain a good share of them:
  1. **The same rule is written several times and the copies have diverged.** Examples: text width (measured 6 ways), reading numbers (over 60 copies in the interface and 4 new readers in the engine), the units of measure in cost and in planning, chord rules for curves (3), the way level marks are written (2).
  2. **One user action sends several commands**, so several undo steps. If a command in the middle fails, what was done before stays (see §3.1).
  3. **The engine's answers are not protected against old answers**, so a slow answer overwrites the new one. The case appears in about 15 dialogs.
- **Fixed values:** about 90 values that belong to the project, the office or the norm. The most important concern the norms: safety factors, deflection limits, kmod, laps, hooks, bolt hole clearances, holidays, the currency and VAT. The project says "the norms and prices are the user's", and these cannot be set.

## 3. The other bugs, by area

### 3.1 One action, several undo steps (cause 2)

- **Core:**
  - restyling joins on walls and beams (`core/index.tsx:162`);
  - the Elevations tool, which does 4 × `view.create` (`core/index.tsx:298`);
  - "Clear all finishes", one command per face (`PaintedFinishesSection.tsx:26`).
- **Studio:** the array, the polar array and the 3D move on a mixed selection (`studio/index.tsx:680-713`).
- **Cost:** "Place on sheet" = a schedule, a sheet and a viewport (`cost/estimate.ts:138`, `cost/schedules.ts:37-83`).
- **Site works:** formwork on a selection, one command per group, and applying a type to several elements (`sitework/index.tsx:75,165`).

The fix: one engine command or an `edit.batch`.

### 3.2 Old answers over new ones (cause 3)

The `live` flag is missing:
- **core:** `ComponentsDialog`, `ExchangeExportDialog`, `LinksDialog`, `FieldAliasesDialog`;
- **viewport:** `TakeoffControls`;
- **cost:** `EstimateView` FormTable, `MapArticlesDialog`, `PriceListsDialog`;
- **rebar:** `partitions`;
- **planning:** `TaskLinksSection`, `CalendarDialog` (the year is asked for at every key: 2, 20, 202, 2027);
- **clash:** `ClashDialog`;
- **graphs:** `openGraph` does not increment the ticket, and two quick saves can lose the second change (`GraphView.tsx:168`).

The fix: a single shared `useEngineQuery(name, args, deps)`.

### 3.3 Engine: the platform

- `ModelCells.cs:125`: `{filter.X.count}` does not count what the view's filter takes. An empty filter counts everything, and the editor shows 0.
- `OutlineHoles.cs:136`: holes are checked against corners and arcs, not ellipses. A hole inside an ellipse is refused.
- `OutlineCuts.cs:77` + `OutlineSplits.cs:77`: a line that cuts an arc twice leaves a piece with 2 corners, refused with "at least three corners".
- `ElementFilter.cs:53-127`: "1,200" means 1.2 for `=` and 1200 for `between`.
- `CoreModule.cs:452`: deleting a storey together with its elements depends on the order of the ids.
- `Provisions.cs:96,150`: an exception from one provider removes all the openings for services, with no message.
- `PaperViews.cs:137`: a `catch (Exception) {}` hides everything a module draws on a sheet.
- **A solid hatch with no fill colour has three rules:** black in the legend, #2b2b2b in 3D, ink in drawings.
- `Sheets.cs:592`: the next sheet number is taken by creation order, so a duplicate can come out. `long.Parse` can overflow.
- `CdeIssues.cs:70`: the next revision is taken by creation order, although `CdeRevisions.Compare` exists.
- `DraftingTables.cs:219`: a formula cell is evaluated twice, so the error "A1 reads itself" also appears twice.
- `StandardsLibrary.cs`: three readers of the same .lin. "DASHED" appears "new" at every import.
- `ArcOutline.cs:176`: a semi-ellipse drawn with 3 collinear corners is refused.
- `Cde.cs:168`: the example name in the settings ignores `CdeHooks.Naming`.
- **Performance:**
  - deleting an instance with a shared body sorts all the entities;
  - copying to storeys makes about 1.25 M calls for 500 walls and 5 storeys;
  - `NameNodes` is O(N·F).

## 5. Fixed values to give to users, by where the setting would live

The rule stays the one in `plan-user-extensibility.md`: the value in the code becomes the default, with the same output.

### 5.1 The project's norm profile (marked "indicative")

- **Concrete** (`ConcreteDesign.cs:10`, `ConcreteMember.cs`):
  - γc 1.5, γs 1.15 and αcc 1.0 exist as parameters but are never passed, although the comment says they can be set;
  - λlim (A 0.7, B 1.1, C 0.7), the imperfection l0/400, e0 = max(h/30, 20 mm);
  - As,max 4 %, As,min, Es 200 GPa (written twice), εcu3 and λ fixed even above C50/60.
- **Steel:**
  - γM0 = γM1 = 1.0 are constants, and γM2 is missing (`SteelDesign.cs:30`);
  - hole clearances, edge distances of 1.5 d0 and a pitch of 3 d0, anchor embedment of 30/25/20 Ø, the default classes 5.6 and 8.8 (`NodeParams.cs:109-143`).
- **Timber** (`TimberDesign.cs`):
  - the deflections L/300 and L/250, although the note shown says the designer chooses them;
  - the table of kmod, kdef, γM 1.3/1.25, km and kcr;

- **Rebar:**
  - the lap of 50Ø fixed in the interface; the commercial lengths of 6 and 12 m; the hook angles 0/90/135/180;
  - the pack's hooks and mandrels, ignored in 6 places;

- **Buckling lengths:** the factors 2.0/0.85/0.7/1.0 by support type, with no default per project (`DesignRun.cs:134`).
- **Stairs and ramps:**
  - the ramp's landing, 1.5 m (`Ramps.cs:53,114`);

### 5.4 The element's type

- **Kitchens** (`KitchenUnits.cs`, `KitchenLayouts.cs`): the 18 mm fronts and carcass, the 3 mm gap, the plinth set back 70/40 mm, the 160 mm top drawer, the 600 × 2150 tall unit, the 1.85 m fridge. Series furniture (Casework) already has parameters for boards; the kitchen does not. Also in Casework, the gap (0.003) and the drawer board (0.016) are constants.
- **Railings:** there is no railing type (§5.1). The bottom rail and the glass are 80 mm, and there are 6 cables.
- **Roof:**
  - the roof window 0.78 × 1.18; the dormer (width 1.6, wall 1.2, slope 15/40°);
  - the fixed sections of the plates, the ridge, the valleys and the purlins; the battens 0.04.
- **Ceiling:** the default height of 2.7 m (`Ceilings.cs:110,298`).
- **Road and path** (`Paths.cs`):
  - `GutterSlab` 0.12 and `SidewalkFall` 0.02 are constants, although the rest of the side is parameterised;
  - there is no superelevation in curves, minimum radius or speed table;
  - the sweep step of 2 m and the design line sampled at 1 m.
- **Scaffold** (`Scaffolds.cs:140`): curved façades are split every 3 m, ignoring the type's bays. The fix is `s.Bays.Max()`.
- **Lift** (`ElevatorBuilder.cs`): the machine room 2.4 m, the hydraulic room 1.6 × 2.0 with 0.15 walls.
- **A new strip foundation** (`StructureTypesDialog.tsx:52-141`): fixed layers with English names (XPS 100, waterproofing 5, protection board 20) and the foundation level of −1.8 m. In the same way, new wall and slab types (`WallTypesDialog.tsx:79`, `SlabTypesDialog.tsx:28`) create the materials in English in a Romanian project.

### 5.5 The application's preferences

- **Snap:**
  - the 10 px aperture (in 5 places), the 6 px selection, the 3 px cycle, closing the polygon at 8 px, parallels and tangents at 40 px;
  - the tracking point after 350 ms, at most 7.
- **The adaptive grid** is metric only: the series 1/5/25/100/500, snap 0.05 or 0.5 m, so an imperial project has 50 mm steps. The 3D grid ignores `gridMajor`.
- **Navigation:** the walk's sensitivity 0.004 rad/px, the wheel's steps 0.5/1.5 m, `WHEEL_STEP` 0.2, the 4–5 px thresholds.
- **Walking through the model:** eye height 1.6, speed 1.4, body 1.8, step 0.35, jump 4.2.
- **The camera:** FOV 45, the far plane 5000, so a large site (Urban) is cut at 5 km.
- **Real size** assumes 96 dpi, with no calibration, so 1:100 on the screen is not 1:100.
- **3D colours:** the background, the grid, the edges 0x1e2a3a and their weight 1.6, the caps' hatch, the terrain. The selection can already be set, the rest cannot.
- **Updates:** a single check 8 s after start, with no interval and no option to turn it off (it matters for managed offices).
- **Lists:** 8 recent projects, 60 results in the command palette.
- **The assistant:** `temperature`, `num_ctx` 16384, `MAX_STEPS` 8 (`num_ctx` matters on machines with little memory).
- **Limits in the engine:**
  - a project script: regeneration 60 s (`EngineHub.cs:614`);
  - a library file: 16 MB;
  - `quantities.many`: 5000 ids, truncated with no message;
  - points in the mesh export: 5 M.
- **Values remembered between uses** differ by module: analysis remembers them, the rest do not (the terrain brush, MEP, a 4 × 3 table, tags, the grid `3*6000`, Studio's values 0.05/3/6/90).

### 5.6 The export dialog's options

- **IFC:**
  - FILE_NAME has an empty author and organisation, although `ProjectInfo` has `author` and `designer`;
  - the unit is always METRE;
  - ViewDefinition is always ReferenceView_V1.2.
- **The clash tolerance** is limited to 2000 mm and always asked for in mm. The report waits 700 ms for each image.
- **Drawing scales:** `shopDrawing.ts`, `callouts.tsx` and `typePreviewState.ts` ignore the project's "Standard scales" list. Paper formats are only A4–A0.
